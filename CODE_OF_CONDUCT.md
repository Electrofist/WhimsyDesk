# Code of Conduct

Desktop Objects is a friendly place for designers, developers and people who just like nice things on their desktop.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people making their first contribution.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit others' ideas and art.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual or violent content.
- Sharing someone's private information.
- Passing off someone else's work as your own.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer, Krrish Parmar, through a GitHub private message to [@Electrofist](https://github.com/Electrofist). Reports are kept private. The maintainer may remove content, close issues or block people who break these rules.

Based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
