Fun things to put on your Mac desktop

Your desktop is the first thing you see when you open your Mac, and usually the messiest. Here are a few ways to make it more fun, from things built into macOS to small free apps.

Built into macOS

  • Widgets on the desktop. On macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Control-click the desktop and choose Edit Widgets. Drag a clock, the weather, a photo or your calendar right onto the desktop.
  • Moving wallpapers. In System Settings, then Wallpaper, pick one of the aerial landscapes. They slowly move when you unlock your Mac.
  • Stacks. Control-click the desktop and choose Use Stacks to turn loose files into neat piles. More tidy than fun, but it clears space for everything else.

Objects that hold your files

WhimsyDesk is a set of free, open source apps that put real-feeling objects on your desktop. Each one is also a place to keep files:

  • A fridge with a heavy door that swings open, cold fog, and frost on files you leave too long. You can stick notes and photo magnets on the door.
  • A wooden drawer that slides up from the bottom of the screen, with soft wooden knocks.
  • A red post box where a handpicked letter arrives every now and then, including short real letters from history.
  • A vending machine where your files stand on little shelves. Press a button, or type a code on the keypad version, and one drops.

They sit behind your windows, do nothing while you are not touching them, and never track you. Want something different? You can make your own object by drawing two pictures.

Seasonal touches

  • Holiday lights around the menu bar and Dock, from Festivitas.
  • Snow and Christmas lights, plus a wallpaper in the spirit of old Mac OS X, from Snowy, which is free.

Desktop companions

  • Mochi is a small pink creature that wanders your desktop and tucks loose files into folders.
  • Hamdori puts a 3D hamster on your desktop, with a focus timer and toys.

One rule for all of them

Keep it to one or two things. A fun desktop is one you smile at, not one you have to clean up again. If clutter is the real problem, start with how to clean up your Mac desktop.
