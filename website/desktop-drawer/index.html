Desktop Drawer

A wooden drawer under your desktop for everything you are not using right now.

A worn wooden drawer with a cup pull that slides out from the bottom of the screen, with weight, bumpers and soft wooden sounds. Put Back returns files to where they were.

Download Desktop DrawerView the source

How it works

  • A wooden drawer along the bottom of your screen. Slide it out, drop files in, push it shut.
  • Weight. Friction, soft bumpers and a little bounce when it hits the stop.
  • Sound. Wooden knocks and a sliding rumble that follows your hand.
  • Put Back. Files go back to their exact spot on your desktop.

Install

  1. Download the .dmg and open it.
  2. Drag the app into Applications and open it from there.
  3. The first time, macOS may say it cannot be opened, because the app is not registered with Apple yet. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
