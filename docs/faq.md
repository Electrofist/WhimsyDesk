# FAQ

## Where do my files go?

Into a normal folder in your home folder. The fridge uses `~/Fridge`, the drawer uses `~/Desktop Drawer`, the mailbox uses `~/Mailbox`, the vending machines use `~/Vending Machine` and `~/Vending Machine Keypad`. Right-click the object and choose the "Open ... Folder" item to see them in Finder.

## Does it remember my files after a restart?

Yes. Each object keeps a small list of what it holds and where each file came from, so Put Back still works after you quit, restart or update.

## If I quit or delete the app, are my files gone?

No. They stay in the folder above until you move them.

## Why does macOS ask to let it control Finder?

To put a file's icon back in the exact spot it had on your desktop. If you say no, everything still works; Put Back just lets Finder choose the spot.

## macOS says the app cannot be opened

The apps are not notarized by Apple yet. Double-click once and close the warning, then open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

## Does it drain my battery?

No. When nobody is touching an object, it runs no timers and does no polling. Animation only runs while something moves.

## How do I quit?

Right-click the object and choose Quit.

## Windows or Linux?

No. The objects are native macOS apps and need macOS 14 or later.
