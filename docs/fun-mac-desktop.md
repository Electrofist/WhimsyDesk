# Fun things to put on your Mac desktop

Your desktop is the first thing you see when you open your Mac, and usually the messiest. Here are a few ways to make it more fun, from things built into macOS to small free apps.

## Built into macOS

- **Widgets on the desktop.** On macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Control-click the desktop and choose **Edit Widgets**. Drag a clock, the weather, a photo or your calendar right onto the desktop.
- **Moving wallpapers.** In **System Settings**, then **Wallpaper**, pick one of the aerial landscapes. They slowly move when you unlock your Mac.
- **Stacks.** Control-click the desktop and choose **Use Stacks** to turn loose files into neat piles. More tidy than fun, but it clears space for everything else.

## Objects that hold your files

[WhimsyDesk](https://whimsydesk.art) is a set of free, open source apps that put real-feeling objects on your desktop. Each one is also a place to keep files:

- **[A fridge](https://whimsydesk.art/desktop-fridge/)** with a heavy door that swings open, cold fog, and frost on files you leave too long. You can stick notes and photo magnets on the door.
- **[A wooden drawer](https://whimsydesk.art/desktop-drawer/)** that slides up from the bottom of the screen, with soft wooden knocks.
- **[A red post box](https://whimsydesk.art/desktop-mailbox/)** where a handpicked letter arrives every now and then, including short real letters from history.
- **[A vending machine](https://whimsydesk.art/desktop-vending-machine/)** where your files stand on little shelves. Press a button, or type a code on the [keypad version](https://whimsydesk.art/desktop-vending-machine-keypad/), and one drops.

They sit behind your windows, do nothing while you are not touching them, and never track you. Want something different? You can [make your own object](https://whimsydesk.art/make-your-own/) by drawing two pictures.

## Seasonal touches

- **Holiday lights** around the menu bar and Dock, from [Festivitas](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/11/25/festivitas-holiday-app/).
- **Snow and Christmas lights,** plus a wallpaper in the spirit of old Mac OS X, from [Snowy](https://9to5mac.com/2025/11/30/snowy-adds-christmas-lights-snow-and-an-aqua-inspired-wallpaper-to-your-mac-for-free/), which is free.

## Desktop companions

- **[Mochi](https://www.producthunt.com/products/mochi-6)** is a small pink creature that wanders your desktop and tucks loose files into folders.
- **[Hamdori](https://www.producthunt.com/products/hamdori)** puts a 3D hamster on your desktop, with a focus timer and toys.

## One rule for all of them

Keep it to one or two things. A fun desktop is one you smile at, not one you have to clean up again. If clutter is the real problem, start with [how to clean up your Mac desktop](clean-up-mac-desktop.md).
